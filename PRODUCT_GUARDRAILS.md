# Scrimnet product guardrails

Scrimnet exists to help legitimate collegiate esports teams find, schedule, coordinate, and complete practice scrims.

The product optimizes for reliable practice—not competitive status, public performance, or a competitive ecosystem.

## Permanent exclusions

Do not build these features for Scrimnet:

- Rankings or rating systems
- Ladders
- Tournaments or tournament hosting
- Wins/losses leaderboards
- Detailed match scores
- Player statistics
- Team performance statistics

Reliability data is allowed only when it helps teams decide whether an opponent will honor a scheduled scrim:

- Show-up rate
- Confirmed/completed scrims
- Check-ins
- Late cancellations
- No-shows

Any new feature idea belongs in a future review only if it supports practice scheduling, coordination, safety, or reliability.
